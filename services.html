Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you order - anything else, just ask on Email or WhatsApp.

How do I order a service?

Pick a gig below, then order by Email, WhatsApp, or directly on Fiverr where the gig is listed. You get a confirmed price and delivery date before any work starts - no payment surprises.

What do you need from me to start?

Your requirements, plus server or domain access for the job - for example your VPS login, domain name, or app repository. I confirm the plan first and flag anything that needs fixing before setup.

How fast is delivery?

Basic packages start from 2 days; larger Standard and Premium work takes 3 to 14 days depending on scope. Exact delivery times are shown on every gig, and Fiverr gigs also offer fast delivery.

Do you offer support after delivery?

Yes - every gig includes revisions (1 to 6 depending on package) and a written guide where it applies. The business email server gig includes 7 days of free support for work-related issues.

Can you work on my existing server or cloud?

Yes. I work on your VPS, Hetzner, DigitalOcean, AWS, or your own hardware - including Proxmox, VMware, OpenStack, and MikroTik networks already running in production.
